About RouteSaathi

College project demo. Major Indian corridors use verified train numbers, airline codes, station/IATA codes, and realistic fare slabs, with direct pre-filled booking links to IRCTC, ixigo, ConfirmTkt, Google Flights, MakeMyTrip, redBus, AbhiBus, and Google Maps (plus optional RapidAPI live IRCTC integration). When online, place search and road routes use OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors) through Photon and OSRM, and satellite imagery is from Esri. Fonts: Alegreya, Cinzel & Noto Serif Devanagari (SIL Open Font License). Not connected to IRCTC, Google or any booking company.
Built by Arpit Ganeshaanand (25BCON2339), Madhur Kumar (25BCON2463) & Sarthak Gupta (25BCON2424) · Prompt Engineering Final Project (3rd Semester), B.Tech CSE, JECRC University, Jaipur. Powered by Gemini AI Chatbot, OpenStreetMap & OSRM.
